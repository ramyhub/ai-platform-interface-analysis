This repository contains screenshots documenting the user interfaces of web-based conversational AI (CAI) platforms, collected as supporting materials for the research paper "Perceived but Not Provided: Auditing Privacy Expectations and Interface-Level Governance in Conversational AI."
About the Study

This study examines the relationship between users' privacy concerns and the privacy and data governance mechanisms provided through conversational AI platform interfaces.

The research combines two complementary methods:

Reddit analysis: Analysis of 27,342 privacy-related posts from 20 conversational AI communities to identify users' privacy concerns and expectations.

Interface walkthroughs: Structured walkthroughs of 10 web-based conversational AI platforms to examine observable privacy controls, data management features, and interface design practices.

The screenshots represent platform interfaces at the time of collection. Interfaces and available controls may have changed.

The analysis focuses on observable interface features and does not independently verify backend data processing, retention, or deletion practices.

This repository provides supporting interface documentation and does not contain the Reddit dataset analyzed in the study.

Associated Paper

Perceived but Not Provided: Auditing Privacy Expectations and Interface-Level Governance in Conversational AI.

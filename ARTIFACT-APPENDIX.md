## Description

This artifact accompanies our PoPETs 2027 paper. It contains screenshots documenting the interfaces of 10 web-based conversational AI platforms and an Excel evaluation checklist. These materials support the paper's interface walkthrough.

### Security/Privacy Issues and Ethical Concerns

The screenshots document platform interfaces as they appeared at the time of collection. The released materials are screenshots and a spreadsheet. They can be inspected without running code or signing into the platforms.
